The following upstream data files are embedded without modification in SakuraTranslate.dll:
STPhrases.txt, STCharacters.txt, TWPhrasesIT.txt, TWPhrasesName.txt,
TWPhrasesOther.txt, TWVariants.txt.
The OpenCC license is also embedded as SakuraTranslate.Resources.OpenCC.LICENSE.txt.

TaiwanChineseConverter.cs is a new managed C# implementation for .NET 3.5,
following OpenCC longest-prefix segmentation and the s2twp conversion stages.
It additionally preserves angle-bracket markup and brace placeholders.
The package does not distribute or require the native OpenCC executable/library.
